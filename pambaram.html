Pambaram

'Pambaram' (Spinning top in Malayalam Language)

A stool to break the monotony of simply sitting.

The furniture is made by Thomas James, in his first semester during his Bachelors (specializing in Furniture and Interior Design) at National Institute of Design, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. It is made with teak wood with linseed oil finishing. Designed for the people who have to work for long, continuous hours to interrupt their boredom and
ease their lives and feel like a child again!

Really grateful to him for letting me capture his efforts, I have personally always loved his designs - the constant idea of breaking the traditional methods of using things!
A special mention and thanks to Samruddhi Bhanage, who is also a Furniture and Interior Design student at NID, Ahmedabad, for modelling and helping us in the project. Absolutely appreciate their hardwork and hope you feel the same way, happy scrolling!
