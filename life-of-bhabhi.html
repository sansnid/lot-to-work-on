Life of bhabhi

‘Watch’. Happy watching!

I shooted this character sketch in my 4th semester while doing my Bachelors of Design at National Institute of Design, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

After spending an entire day, walking and sketching through the narrow, intricately carved lanes of Amdavad, I met my main character - Yasmin Bhabhi!
The meeting is still fresh in my memories, the bold iconic entry of Bhabhi telling me not to worry and how girls should never be scared of travelling alone for their work and future! The next 3 days with her were some of the best days of my life.

I have tried to capture a tiny essence of Bhabhi’s life in my film, hope it reaches you well!
As for myself, I look forward of going back and spending another pleasant afternoon with her, while she peacefully sings a soothing retro-bollywood song :) You can watch the film ‘here’. Happy watching!
